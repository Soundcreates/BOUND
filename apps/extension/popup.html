BOUND

BOUND reviews supported wallet requests and opens a warning before they reach your wallet. It scores and warns; it never refuses a request. A grey verdict means there is no baseline to compare.

MonitoringMonitoring is off.

While monitoring is off, BOUND checks nothing, records nothing, reads no trust data and sends no reports. Wallet requests go straight to your wallet.
