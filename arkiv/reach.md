# Reach and limits

Arkiv stores public site-level observations and watcher-owned baseline, drift, incident, and status records. Only watcher-owned records accepted after owner and expiry checks may inform those signals. Any party can publish a lookalike schema. Observations and reporter counts cannot establish trust. Reporter keys are rotating pseudonyms, not verified distinct people; Sybil resistance is absent.

The extension must download seeded records as a bundle. Per-site and uncurated-site reads need the specified Anon-RPC route. The watcher or public nodes may see network metadata. No live extension-to-Arkiv round trip is demonstrated yet.
